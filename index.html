● VAL-TRAD-02 / HISTORICAL TESTS

A satellite sees a thermal signal — not a confirmed fire.

MODIS and VIIRS see the landscape with different pixel sizes and at different times. Two nearby observations may be related, but neither location nor a similar temperature proves a single fire.

The historical comparison adjusts detection counts statistically in pilot regions A/B. This is different from the November 2026 observation-level Eagle's View case.

In a separate Amazonas study, the global method's held-out WAPE worsened from 23.11% in 2024 to 50.73% in 2025. It is not a reliable universal physical conversion.
